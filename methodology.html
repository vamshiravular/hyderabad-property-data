Methodology

What each figure means, how future data will be reviewed, and why the limitations matter.

Important context

Version 1 has zero reviewed listing observations. All displayed prices, rents, examples, and yield percentages are fictional demonstrations. No numerical figure on this site represents a verified current Hyderabad market range.

Five different kinds of value

Listing or asking sale price
A seller’s published request; it may change and is not the final price paid.
Transaction price
The amount agreed for a completed sale, where a reliable record is available. Fees and other costs may be separate.
Rental asking price
The advertised monthly amount requested by an owner or agent.
Achieved rent
The final monthly rent agreed in a signed lease; it can differ from an advertised amount.
Government guideline or registration value
An official value used in the registration context. It is not necessarily a current negotiated transaction price. Check the official Market Value Search for the relevant property details.

How future observations would be handled

Property listings can be duplicated across portals, stay online after they are outdated, quote different area measurements, or contain data-entry errors and extreme outliers. A future real-data release should record the source and collection date, deduplicate listings, normalize units and property type, review suspicious entries, and disclose how exclusions were made.

We would prefer medians and clearly defined ranges over a simple average, because one unusually expensive listing can distort an average. Each published range should state its property type, sample size, source dates, geography, definition of floor area, and last review date. If a sample is too small or inconsistent, we should publish no range.

Initial real-data updates would be monthly or quarterly after manual review. There is no automated scraping in Version 1.

Gross rental yield

Gross rental yield (%) = (monthly rent × 12 ÷ property value) × 100

This is a simplified pre-expense estimate. It does not account for maintenance, vacancy, repairs, taxes, loan interest, registration expenses, brokerage, or other ownership costs. A sample yield displayed elsewhere is illustrative and is not calculated from a pair of independent ranges.

Limits and verification

This site cannot guarantee prices, rents, appreciation, occupancy, or investment returns. Independently check the Telangana RERA registered project or agent search and the relevant official registration information. Consult qualified legal, tax, or financial professionals for your circumstances.
